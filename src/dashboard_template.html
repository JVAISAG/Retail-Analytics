Metric definitions

  • Gross sales: positive merchandise transaction value.
  • Returns: absolute value of negative merchandise transaction lines, on the recorded date.
  • Net sales: gross sales minus returns; excludes shipping and nonstandard product codes.
  • Orders: distinct positive-sale invoices.
  • Repeat buyer: identified customer with at least two distinct positive-sale invoices.
  • RFM: recency in calendar days; frequency in invoices; monetary in gross merchandise GBP.

Customer metrics exclude anonymous sales. Net transaction value is not profit or audited accounting revenue.

Study design and limitations

First observed purchases may predate this dataset. Cohorts reflect observation, rather than verified acquisition. Customer mix includes wholesale buyers. No inventory, cost, marketing-spend or randomized treatment data is available.

RFM scores use average percentile ranks with ties preserved. R and F define the segment; M prioritizes within it. Labels such as “At risk” describe a rule, not a trained churn probability.
